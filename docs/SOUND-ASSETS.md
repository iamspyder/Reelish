# Reelish sound assets

Reelish includes 12 original procedural effects and 100 bundled sounds from Kenney's **Interface Sounds 1.0**. The names and counts describe Reelish's library, not Captik's catalog.

Kenney source: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds

Official archive: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/interface-sounds/fa43c1dd4d-1677589452/kenney_interface-sounds.zip

The official page identifies Creative Commons CC0. The archive's included license explicitly permits personal, educational and commercial use. The original OGG files and license are retained in `src/assets/sounds/kenney-interface/`; `catalog.json` records their measured durations. Kenney attribution is appreciated and supplied here, although the license does not require it. Retrieved 27 October 2026.

Automatic placement uses transparent timing/keyword suggestions from caption text. It is reviewed before application and does not invoke an AI service. The imported sound files remain on the user's device. Per-event source trim, duration, level and fades apply to preview and final audio mixing.
