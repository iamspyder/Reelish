Reelish is here: offline automatic captions, expressive styles, and a local video-editing workflow.

- Included multilingual Whisper Base captioning — no key, account, separate model download or service-minute quota.
- 136 caption presets and 37 bundled font families, with font imports, favorites and saved looks.
- Word timing and emphasis editing, pause cutting, motion keyframes, person-depth captions and subject framing.
- Background music, 12 procedural sound effects and imported audio.
- Watermark-free MP4 export up to 4K; SRT, WebVTT and ASS subtitle export.
- Local project saving, autosave recovery and undo/redo.

Choose Windows x64, macOS Apple Silicon, macOS Intel, or Linux x64 below. Downloaded packages include their offline speech model and video tools. Checksums are provided beside each file.

Community builds are unsigned and macOS builds are not Apple-notarized; your operating system may ask you to review the application before opening it. Linux AppImages may require FUSE compatibility libraries; a Debian/Ubuntu package is also provided.

Recognition accuracy, masking quality and rendering speed depend on your media and hardware. Review captions before publishing. This release does not include mobile apps, automatic translation, generative B-roll or professional-editor plugins.

Legacy .clipora projects remain readable. Reelish uses its own application settings directory.
